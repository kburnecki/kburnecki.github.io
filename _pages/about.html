I am an Associate Professor at the Faculty of Pure and Applied Mathematics, Wrocław University of Science and Technology, where I head the Department of Applied Mathematics and serve as Vice-director of the Hugo Steinhaus Center.

My research connects stochastic modelling and computational statistics with applications in insurance mathematics and mathematical (bio)physics, with a particular interest in heavy-tailed and long-memory models.
