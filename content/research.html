Stochastic modelling

My research includes heavy-tailed and long-memory models, self-similar stochastic processes and (tempered) fractional dynamical systems. These models describe variability and dependence beyond classical Gaussian and short-memory assumptions.

Computational statistics & mathematical (bio)physics

I work on statistical identification and validation of stochastic dynamics, including anomalous diffusion and single-particle tracking. Applications include complex physical and biological systems.

Insurance mathematics

My work in insurance mathematics covers loss distributions, risk processes, ruin probabilities and insurance-linked securities.
