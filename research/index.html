Research

Stochastic models, statistical methods and their applications.

Applied mathematics at the interface of stochastic processes, statistical inference and real-world data.

International cooperation →Conferences and talks →

Mathematical physics and biophysics

I develop stochastic models of anomalous diffusion and complex physical and biological systems. My work combines fractional dynamics, heavy-tailed processes and statistical analysis of single-particle trajectories to explain heterogeneous motion, membrane receptor diffusion and receptor interactions.

Selected publications and related grants →

Actuarial mathematics and risk management

My research in actuarial mathematics covers insurance loss models, risk processes, ruin probabilities and annuities under random interest rates. I also study natural catastrophe risk and the valuation of insurance-linked securities, including multi-peril catastrophe bonds and contingent convertible catastrophe bonds.

Selected publications and related grants →

Statistical methods and time series modelling

I develop methods for identifying, estimating and validating stochastic processes and time series, with emphasis on long memory, heavy tails and (tempered) fractional dynamical systems. Applications include single-particle tracking, measurement errors, solar variability and distinguishing between competing models of anomalous diffusion.
